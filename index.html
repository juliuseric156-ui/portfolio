Retail demand

Supermarket Sales & Demand

Staples contributed 46.1% of recorded revenue. Sunflower cooking oil was the top individual product at TZS 507,395. Use these results to review which products need closer stock monitoring.

Methods and limitations

The report aggregates workbook revenue by product and category, groups units sold into complete Monday–Sunday weeks, and checks 100 transactions using 1.5×IQR limits. It presents a next-full-week projection for black tea, laundry soap, and rice. The report does not document the forecast model or its historical accuracy.

Sales and conversion

Electronics Shop Sales

The five top-selling products accounted for 67% of 523 units across 100 orders. Use the ranking and channel breakdown to decide which products to review first for stock availability.

Methods and limitations

Revenue is quantity ordered multiplied by unit price and then summed by product. The next-quarter mix assumes Q4 2023 shares carry forward. Inquiry and social account fields are simulated. The report gives no ad spend or currency, so it cannot estimate advertising cost per sale or return on ad spend.
